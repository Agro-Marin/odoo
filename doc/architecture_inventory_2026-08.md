# Framework Core — Per-File Inventory (2026-08)

Companion to `architecture_audit_2026-08.md`. Every non-addon file under
`odoo/`, its size, its module-docstring summary, and cheap structural signals.
Generated by AST walk; regenerate rather than hand-edit.

Scope note: `odoo/` contains **1033** `.py` files / **335 kLOC** in total, but
**502** of those files are the bundled addons under `odoo/addons/`. The
*framework core* — what this audit is about — is the 531 files / 126 kLOC below.
